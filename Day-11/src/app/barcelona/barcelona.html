FC Barcelona

FC Barcelona is a football club from Barcelona, Spain. It was founded in 1899.

The club's motto is "Mes que un club" which means "More than a club".

FC Barcelona plays its home games at Camp Nou, one of the biggest stadiums in Europe.

The club has won many trophies over the years, including 5 Champions League titles and 27 La Liga titles.

Some of the most famous players who played for Barcelona are Lionel Messi, Andres Iniesta, Xavi and Ronaldinho.
